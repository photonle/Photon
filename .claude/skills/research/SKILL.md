---
name: research
description: Investigate a question against high-trust primary sources and post the findings as a comment on its Research issue. Use when the user wants a topic researched, docs or API facts gathered, or reading legwork delegated to a background agent.
---

Spin up a **background agent** to do the research, so you keep working while it reads.

Its job:

1. Investigate the question against **primary sources** (official docs, source code, specs, first-party APIs, the Garry's Mod wiki), not a secondary write-up of them. Follow every claim back to the source that owns it.
2. Write the findings as Markdown, citing each claim's source with a link.
3. Post them as a comment on the question's Research issue in `photonle/Photon`, opening with the AI disclaimer `> *This was generated by AI during research.*`, then close the issue as completed. Where the question has no issue yet, file one first: type Research, per [the `github-issues` skill](../github-issues/SKILL.md).

A comment holds at most 65,536 characters. Findings that don't fit are either **condensed** (keep the answer, the claims a decision rests on and their citations; cut worked detail, long quotes and dead ends, and say at the end what was cut) or **split** across consecutive comments, each headed `Part n of m`, with the answer in the first. Condense where the detail is padding; split where it is load-bearing.
